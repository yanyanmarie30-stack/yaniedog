Essential Dog Care 🐾

Every dog deserves a healthy, safe, and loving home. Here are the essentials for keeping your furry friend happy.

✓

🥣 Healthy Food and Water

Provide balanced meals and fresh, clean water.

✓

🦴 Regular Exercise

Keep your dog active with walks and playtime.

✓

🛁 Proper Grooming

Maintain a clean coat, nails, and healthy skin.

✓

🩺 Veterinary Care

Schedule checkups and recommended vaccinations.

✓

🏡 Safe Shelter

Offer a comfortable, clean place to rest.

✓

🎓 Love and Training

Use patience, positive reinforcement, and kindness.
